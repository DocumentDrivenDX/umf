# Successor006 — one original grant, per-attempt receipt, cumulative campaign

Proposed design clarification for root and external review. Proposal005's `priorCampaignCharge` called a CampaignCharge through confirmed preparation without distinguishing a cumulative original checkpoint from the successful phase1 receipt's ONE-grant charge. Its common decision journal and outer campaign could also be misread as two grant allocators. This document supersedes those ambiguous interpretations. All80 predecessor acceptance rows and full F1/F2/F3 scope remain required; no code, original durable store, native outcome, signature scope or capability is implemented or qualified here.

## Three distinct values and the exact formula

1. A native `budgetReceipt.campaignCharge` describes that ONE dispatched attempt. In successful phase2's existing phaseReceipt, or denied phase2's unchanged negative outcome, `elapsedBudgetMs` MUST EQUAL the SAME original dispatched common grant's `reservedMs`. It is NEVER the cumulative charge or the sum of earlier phase2 attempts. Existing component counters/receipt shapes remain exact. Native work/COMMIT cannot exceed that charged grant and original signed timer/deadline; a pre-COMMIT receipt never invents future measured elapsed time.
2. Common decision accounting's `priorCampaignCharge` is the COMPLETE cumulative original outer checkpoint's CampaignCharge at the boundary BEFORE the first phase2 allocation. It includes ALL preparation attempt grants, including positively aborted attempts and any previously unknown attempt later resolved to noncommit/commit. It is NOT the phase1 success receipt's per-attempt CampaignCharge, a caller zero, the latest receipt, or a reconstruction from successful native receipts alone. The full original checkpoint/grants/proofs remain in actual original private custody. An unresolved preparation attempt blocks phase2; its grant remains charged nevertheless.
3. The authoritative current outer checkpoint counts each original campaign grant ONCE, across ALL phases and ALL outcomes. Let `G_k` be its original ordered grant prefix at checkpoint k. `CumulativeMs(k) = SUM(g.reservedMs for UNIQUE allocation keys in G_k)`. For phase2 common accounting after n dispatched attempts: `chargedMs = priorCampaignCharge.elapsedBudgetMs + SUM(commonGrant[i].reservedMs for i=1..n)`. At the same original phase2 checkpoint, these two expressions MUST be equal. Future business admission uses `campaignBudgetMs - CumulativeMs(current)` plus all original stricter caps/actual continuous deadlines. It never replaces CumulativeMs with the last successful phaseReceipt or a fresh decision journal.

Every arithmetic operation is exact nonnegative safe-integer arithmetic excluding booleans, <=900000 signed campaignBudgetMs and all tighter original limits. Unknown, aborted, committed and dispatched grants all contribute their FULL reservedMs; status changes contribute ZERO additional time. Replaying any native receipt, native observation, private settlement, common journal or campaign checkpoint contributes ZERO. A positively proved original abort followed by a permitted new attempt contributes EXACTLY that NEW grant, without subtracting the old one. Unknown already occupies/charges its original grant and permits NO new attempt. No alternative bootstrap/campaign/slot/identity can evade an occupied original installation campaign key.

## ONE authoritative durable allocation, not two reconciled budgets

Select ONE originally enrolled fixed campaign custodian/provider branch under the signed metadata/provider definition, SAME original installation writer lease and original durable storage transaction. It owns BOTH the outer campaign checkpoint and the common decision journal. `reserve_and_mark_dispatched(originalCampaign,originalPhase,originalAttempt)` is one exact private fixed operation, not a public callback/append/SQL/JSON API. BEFORE first phase1 dispatch this actual provider must admit the new campaign branch under original context/setup custody. Existing owner-ledger/store wire is NOT enlarged or reinterpreted. If the original provider cannot support this exact same-store atomic transaction and durable compare-and-reserve, ALL affected inventory preparation/decision effects refuse and enablement stays OPEN. No cross-store eventual reconciliation or second independent allocator is an admitted substitute.

For phase2 the ONE durable transaction validates original common request/reservation and actual prior checkpoint, requires the common next monotonic attempt number and full remaining cap, then atomically: (a) records ONE immutable common grant under its allocation key, (b) appends the outer entry referring to THAT SAME complete grant, (c) marks the SAME common attempt dispatched and appends that grant digest to its accounting, (d) advances the outer cumulative charge and common chargedMs to the SAME exact value and (e) advances original custody sequences. These are views/references to ONE authoritative reservation, not two debits. No state permits an externally dispatchable grant in only one view. A new phase1/non-decision attempt performs the same outer allocation/CAS without inventing a common decision slot.

The actual provider's expected-prefix CAS and durable original allocation-key uniqueness decide allocation. It returns success only after this entire transaction is positively durable. A lost CAS response forbids native effects or another allocation until the SAME original key/prefix/lease/private store transaction is resolved. If committed, reattach the exact original grant/entries/accounting with ZERO charge; this is NOT permission to execute an already-dispatched handle again. Its original native/driver dispatch status must independently resolve under005's pending/unknown/noncommit rules before any effect. If positively not committed, retry the SAME original intended allocation under the same key after full original remaining checks, never infer noncommit from a missing file/row alone. Partial/mismatching views, digest/key collision, missing primary/history or a store restore/rewind REFUSE as corruption; they are not repaired by summing submitted receipts, fabricating a checkpoint, allocating another decisionId or creating a sidecar.

Later outcome settlement changes entry state/proof/outcome and common journal state/settlement in the SAME original durable transaction where both views change; it does NOT append another grant or increase elapsedBudgetMs. Native-confirmed/private-pending recovery verifies the actual original native binding/outcome first, settles the SAME dispatched grant and request once, and preserves charge. Terminal/read-only replay cannot dispatch, allocate, append or recharge. Even when no original effects are possible during recovery, missing original proof keeps mutable capabilities fenced.

## Exact native/common/outer correspondence and acyclic prefix

Use the EXISTING native `decisionBinding` `{decisionId,requestDigest,reservationDigest,attemptNumber,attemptGrantDigest,decision}`. Together with the actual original request's phaseId and campaign binding it resolves EXACTLY ONE outer identity and the SAME common grant. No new field, external ApprovalReceipt or signature scope is added to native approval/decisionBinding. Approved phase2 uses existing `phaseReceipt.budgetReceipt`; denied uses unchanged `approvalOutcome.budgetReceipt`, never a successful phase2 receipt. In both, `campaignCharge.elapsedBudgetMs=grant.reservedMs`, policyBinding equals the grant's complete original binding, and source/phase/native timer/purpose/context/census/COMMIT proof remain exact. Outer/common totals are independently derived from original grant history, not copied from that one native charge.

Common accounting's priorCampaignCharge is frozen from the original cumulative preparation checkpoint AFTER all preparation attempts resolved and actual phase1 COMMIT confirmed, BEFORE any phase2 grant. Its complete privately retained checkpoint must prove every originally allocated preparation key, including positively aborted attempts; a native phase1 receipt alone is insufficient. The original checkpoint is linked by existing common grant.priorAccountingDigest: `preparationCheckpoint -> initial common accounting (empty phase2 grants) -> common grant -> next common accounting + outer checkpoint`. No own/future digest is added. The initial accounting privately retains the complete original preparation checkpoint; its visible priorCampaignCharge equals that checkpoint.cumulativeCharge exactly. At phase2, original outer entries after that checkpoint correspond BIJECTIVELY, in dispatch order, to common grantDigests/attempts. No extra phase/key/grant/native outcome or missing common grant is admitted. This freezes005's earlier abstract prior CampaignCharge source without changing the common wire record.

After settlement, future business grants append to the SAME outer original campaign. They do not alter the frozen common decision accounting/settlement digest. Common chargedMs denotes its terminal phase2 prefix; current outer cumulativeCharge may be higher after actual later phases. A historical phase2 receipt or common settlement replay MUST NOT replace that current checkpoint or decrement its cumulative total. Original historical checkpoints/settlements remain read-only evidence, with actual current installation/source/history custody as005 requires.

Example: preparation attempt1 reserves120ms and positively aborts; preparation attempt2 reserves120ms and commits. The phase1 success receipt charge is120ms, while the original preparation checkpoint and common priorCampaignCharge are240ms. Phase2 attempt1 reserves80ms and positively aborts; attempt2 reserves80ms and commits/denies. That final native phase2 budget charge is80ms; common chargedMs and outer checkpoint are400ms. Replaying its receipt/settlement remains400ms. A later business grant of150ms makes outer current550ms; historical common settlement still400ms and its replay addsZERO. If phase2 attempt2 is unknown instead,400ms remains charged and no attempt3/business is admitted until original outcome resolves. With original budget450ms, the150ms business grant refuses before dispatch; latest120/80ms receipt substitution would incorrectly admit it and MUST fail.
