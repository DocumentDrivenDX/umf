# Water Quality Portal

## Source

- URL: [Water Quality Portal](https://www.waterqualitydata.us/beta/portal_userguide/)
- Accessed: 2026-10-08. Release/snapshot pin and selected dataset reuse terms remain design inputs unless a release is named below.

## Summary

The Water Quality Portal combines published discrete chemical, physical and biological monitoring data using Water Quality Exchange (WQX) profiles.

## Relevant Findings

The WQX 3 profile guide distinguishes locations, activities and result profiles, with biological taxonomy/counts and analytical metadata. Detection/quantitation-limit information and result-level qualifiers must remain recoverable.

## HELIX Usage

Grounds FEAT-024, US-075 and SD-024 ecology/water-management planning. Source vocabulary/code extraction must use an archived release and explicit mapping tooling; no live vocabulary list is copied into these plans.

## Authority Boundary

Discrete water-quality and biological sample profiles. A narrow export alone cannot substantiate preservation of omitted metadata.
