# Madaba Plains Project publication series

## Source

- URL: [Madaba Plains Project publication series](https://andrewsarchaeology.org/publications/madaba-plains-project-publication-series/)
- Accessed: 2026-10-08. Selected record snapshots and media terms require inventory.

## Summary

Andrews University's Institute of Archaeology publication page identifies Madaba Plains Project seasonal volumes and their documentary content.

## Relevant Findings

The series offers download links and describes photos, maps, plans/drawings, pottery plates, locus summaries and specialist reports. A downloadable volume is not automatically a structured dataset or an unrestricted license for every plate/image.

## HELIX Usage

Grounds FEAT-025, US-076 and SD-025. Extract native vocabulary and identifiers from pinned source bytes through declared mapping tooling, not from remembered labels.

## Authority Boundary

Report/media inventory candidates. Record volume, page/figure/plate, author and actual asset terms for selected evidence.
