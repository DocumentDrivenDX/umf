# EPA aquatic survey data

## Source

- URL: [EPA aquatic survey data](https://www.epa.gov/national-aquatic-resource-surveys/data-national-aquatic-resource-surveys)
- Accessed: 2026-10-08. Release/snapshot pin and selected dataset reuse terms remain design inputs unless a release is named below.

## Summary

EPA National Aquatic Resource Surveys publish survey data and companion field metadata for aquatic-resource assessments.

## Relevant Findings

Published rivers/streams tables include biological and physical measurements. Companion metadata must accompany selected tables; sampled biological observations and published derived condition metrics are different deliverables.

## HELIX Usage

Grounds FEAT-024, US-075 and SD-024 ecology/water-management planning. Source vocabulary/code extraction must use an archived release and explicit mapping tooling; no live vocabulary list is copied into these plans.

## Authority Boundary

Benthic-invertebrate and fish-survey fixtures. Survey design and method govern what population/condition interpretations are justified.
